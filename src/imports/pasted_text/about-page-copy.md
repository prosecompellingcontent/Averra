Meet The CEO

Jayla Smith is the Founder of AVERRA, a self made entrepreneur, investor, and business strategist focused on helping beauty professionals build businesses that can grow beyond nonstop labor.

She did not build AVERRA from the outside looking in.

She grew up inside salon and spa environments watching beauty professionals work long hours to build something for themselves while quietly carrying the pressure of keeping the entire business running every day.

Her stepmother owned a salon.
Her father handled the business operations and bookkeeping.

From a young age, she was exposed to both sides of the industry at the same time:
the artistry and the structure behind keeping a business alive.

As she got older, her experience expanded beyond simply growing up around the industry. She worked inside beauty businesses herself, performed services, studied client behavior, and later worked in beauty school admissions where she saw firsthand how many providers entered the industry passionate about beauty but unprepared for the business realities waiting for them afterward.

That pattern became impossible to ignore.

Talented providers were becoming fully booked while still financially overwhelmed.
Schedules looked successful on the outside while exhaustion quietly became normal behind the scenes.

So Jayla spent years studying the parts of the industry most providers were never taught:

earning five certifications alongside her degree while continuing to study how perception influences buying decisions inside the beauty industry.
